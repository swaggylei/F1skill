# Knowledge mode / 知识问答

Cover F1 history and eras, drivers and rivalries, constructors and lineage, circuits and rules, and unusual statistical achievements. Match depth to the question; do not force a full tracking report onto a historical question.

For drivers distinguish personal accomplishments, teammate comparisons, team opportunities and rule-era effects. For teams distinguish constructor records from entrant names and corporate continuity. For historical stories establish date, event and credible sources before drawing a narrative. State disputed interpretations as disputed.

When asked for odd records, explore the discovery families in tracking.md. Define each category and supply evidence, dates and coverage. If asked for current records, browse and state the cutoff. For a historical fixed result, still verify niche claims rather than trusting recalled trivia. Support Chinese, English and paired bilingual answers; retain official English names beside Chinese names where ambiguity matters.
