# Source policy / 来源策略

Discovery entry points (checked 2026-11-17; select the actual season/event):

- [FIA decisions and event documents](https://www.fia.com/documents): race/Sprint/qualifying classifications, stewards decisions and revisions. The landing page can redirect to an older season. A “Final Classification” title does not by itself resolve a known appeal.
- [Formula1.com results](https://www.formula1.com/en/results): official results and archives; use specific session pages and official feature articles for context.
- [STATS F1](https://www.statsf1.com/en/statistiques.aspx): historical cross-checks, chronology and statistical definitions. It is a secondary historical source, not an authority overriding FIA decisions.

Prefer FIA decisions for classification disputes. If official pages conflict, show the conflict and document dates; seek a later superseding decision. Do not select whichever version creates a record. Historical databases and reputable specialist books may supplement missing history; identify edition/date and definitions. News and social posts are discovery leads, not sole proof of a record. Search snippets are not evidence.

For every source retain exact URL, title, publisher, session/event, document ID/version if present, publication/issue time, retrieval time in UTC, fields supported and coverage. Optional content hash helps detect revisions. Store small extracted facts and provenance; do not redistribute copyrighted archives or bypass access restrictions.

Require both event evidence and a historical comparator for historical record claims. If independent cross-checks are unavailable, label the gap; an official event result alone proves the result, not “first in history”. A network failure is unknown availability, not proof that no GP finished.
